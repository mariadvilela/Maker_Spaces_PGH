Creative Maker Spaces
in Pittsburgh



Manchester Craftsmen's Guild
Since 1968, Manchester Craftsmen's Guild has been a Pittsburgh home for art, music, and learning. Each year, hundreds of young people dive into ceramics, photography, digital arts, and design, and thousands more students join in through the Pittsburgh Public Schools. The results speak for themselves: 90% of students earn their high school diplomas, and 85% of those go on to college or other further education. MCG Jazz also keeps the music playing, with a mission to preserve, present, and promote jazz!





Invention Land
Got a big idea? Inventionland is where ideas become real. Their factory uses the latest technology to build prototypes and product samples tailored to each client's needs. They've long worked with corporations and schools, and a special partnership with Davison Inventions means they can help bring customers' new products to life, too.





BNY Fab Lab
At Kamin Science Center's BNY Fab Lab, anyone can create just about anything they can imagine! Join a workshop or class to learn the basics of 3D printing, laser cutting, vinyl cutting, and more, all in Pittsburgh's favorite makerspace. Whether you're building a prototype from scratch or just curious to try something new, the Fab Lab is the place to be.





Open Lab
Open Lab at Hillman is a team-up between Pitt's University Center for Teaching and Learning and the University Library System. Through workshops, classes, consultations, and trainings, it connects the Pitt community with technology for hands-on making and learning. Drop in to try virtual reality, learn to 3D print and 3D scan, or use a vinyl cutter or laser cutter on almost any material.
